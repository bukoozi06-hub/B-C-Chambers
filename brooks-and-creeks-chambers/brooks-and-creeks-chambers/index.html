About the Chambers

A Nigerian Legal Practice Built on Experience & Integrity

Brooks and Creeks Chambers is a Nigerian legal practice registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission in 2022.

The Chambers focuses on providing legal documentation, consultancy and advisory services, with particular emphasis on capital market-related matters.

Under the leadership of its Principal Partner, the firm draws upon extensive professional experience in private legal practice and regulatory service to provide practical legal support and informed consultancy to individuals, businesses and organisations.

⚖ Regulatory service background

Meet the Principal Partner

Sufianu Abdulkarim

Principal Partner, Brooks and Creeks Chambers

Sufianu Abdulkarim is the Principal Partner of Brooks and Creeks Chambers.

1990

He obtained his law degree from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, in 1990 and commenced his professional career in private legal practice, where he practised until 2001.

2001

In 2001, he joined the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), Nigeria. During his service at the Commission, he worked across various departments, including Legal, Investigation and Enforcement.

Later

He subsequently became the Head of the External Relations Department.

2022

Following his voluntary retirement from the Securities and Exchange Commission, Mr Ajenifuja established Brooks and Creeks Chambers, focusing on legal documentation and consultancy.

He also brings substantial professional experience and expertise in the Nigerian capital market and is available for engagement on capital market-related matters.

His professional background combines private legal practice, regulatory experience, investigation, enforcement and institutional relations.
